Account deletion and privacy requests

You can contact the developer to request deletion of your Salutaris account and associated information. The developer can manually delete account information held in the app’s database.

Salutaris currently does not include an in-app account deletion control. If users can create accounts, an in-app way to initiate account deletion should be implemented before App Store submission to meet Apple’s applicable requirements.
