Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Salutaris handles information when choir members use the app.

Last updated: 20.10.2026

1. Who is responsible for Salutaris?

Salutaris is developed and maintained by an independent developer. For questions about this policy or requests concerning personal information, contact zaharinskijvlad@gmail.com.

2. Information handled by the app

Salutaris is an invitation-based app for members of a choir. Based on the app’s current design, it handles the following information:

  • First and last name: provided by a member during registration.
  • Invitation code and member identifier: a shared choir invitation code is entered during registration, and a member identifier is used to associate the member with their account.
  • Telegram username: an optional username a member may provide to help other choir members contact them.
  • Push notification token: a technical token used to route general choir reminders to a member’s device.
  • Sheet music files: files requested from the choir’s music library and downloaded to the member’s device.

The developer has indicated that Salutaris does not use analytics. This statement should be rechecked if analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or other SDKs are added in a future version.

3. How information is used

Information is used to register and identify choir members, provide access to the sheet music library and member directory, display an optional Telegram username to other members, deliver general push reminders about choir concerts and events, and maintain the operation and security of the app.

4. Sheet music and local storage

Sheet music is hosted on a virtual private server (VPS) provided by a third-party hosting provider in Belarus, according to the developer. Files are downloaded to the member’s device when requested and stored locally for access on that device.

According to the developer, the server does not keep a per-member history of which sheet music files were downloaded. Confirm this against the production backend before publishing.

5. Push notifications

Salutaris uses push notifications for general reminders about choir concerts and events. A push notification token is used to support delivery.

Push delivery involves Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and Apple Push Notification service (APNs). These services may process technical information needed to route and deliver notifications under their respective terms and privacy documentation. Salutaris does not use Firebase Analytics, according to the developer.

6. Hosting and third-party services

Account information and sheet music are stored on a VPS provided by a third-party hosting provider in Belarus. The provider’s legal name has not yet been supplied and should be confirmed before publication.

Apple’s push notification infrastructure and Firebase Cloud Messaging may process technical information needed to deliver push notifications. This policy does not claim that these providers receive all information stored by Salutaris.

7. Retention and deletion

Members can request account deletion by contacting the developer at zaharinskijvlad@gmail.com. The developer can manually delete the member’s first and last name, Telegram username, push notification token, and member identifier from the database.

According to the developer, when a member’s access is revoked, the app signs the member out and clears its local cache, including downloaded sheet music. Verify this behavior on the production iOS app. Local data should not be described as remotely deleted from a device unless that action actually occurs.

The precise retention period for account information and server logs must be confirmed by the developer and described accurately here before publication.

Apple requirement to address: Salutaris currently does not have an in-app account deletion control. If users can create accounts, Apple generally requires users to be able to initiate account deletion from within the app. An email contact alone may not satisfy this requirement. Implement and test an appropriate in-app deletion flow before submission.

8. Security

The developer should maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect account information and sheet music against unauthorized access. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep this description only if it accurately reflects the safeguards implemented in the app and on the server.

9. Intended audience

Salutaris is designed for members of a choir. The app’s minimum age and whether children may use it have not yet been specified. Confirm the intended audience and add an accurate statement about children’s information and any applicable safeguards before publication.

10. Privacy requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact the developer to submit a request. The developer may ask for information reasonably needed to verify the request and protect the account.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
